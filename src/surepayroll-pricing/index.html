SurePayroll pricing: Full Service vs No Tax Filing

For five employees, the published monthly math is $64 for Full Service and $40 for No Tax Filing. That is $768 or $480 for twelve months before optional services, state-specific charges, and any quote-only items.

What to confirm before treating the annual total as your bill

  • Number of W-2 employees and 1099 contractors.
  • States and local jurisdictions where filing or registration is required.
  • Whether year-end forms, multi-state payroll, workers’ compensation, benefits or SureHR are included or extra.
  • Whether the selected plan leaves tax filing with SurePayroll or with your business.
  • Any implementation, promotion, renewal or add-on terms in the checkout quote.

FAQ

What is the five-person Full Service example?

$29 plus $7 for each of five employees is $64 per month, or $768 for twelve months.

What is the five-person No Tax Filing example?

$20 plus $4 for each of five employees is $40 per month, or $480 for twelve months.

Does this include every employer payroll cost?

No. It is a subscription subtotal. State charges, optional services, wages, employer taxes and internal time remain separate or pending.
