QuickBooks vs ADP payroll: choose by scope, not an invented price

There is no verified current ADP dollar price to put beside QuickBooks here. QuickBooks publishes plan and offer terms; ADP RUN presents package scope and asks small businesses to get pricing. The useful decision is which operating scope you need and which quote inputs must be confirmed.

Do not call an ADP quote-only page “cheaper” or “more expensive.” The missing number is a pending quote, not zero.

Quote checklist before comparing annual cost

  • Record company count, worker types, pay frequency and jurisdictions.
  • Separate payroll processing from tax filing, garnishment, SUI, time and attendance, retirement, workers’ compensation and HR add-ons.
  • For QuickBooks, record promotion months and regular months separately.
  • For ADP, request the written monthly base, per-worker or per-run unit, implementation fee, renewal basis and every add-on.

FAQ

Does ADP publish a current small-business price on the checked page?

No. The checked RUN package page presents “Get pricing” and a quote flow; it does not provide a verified dollar amount.

Which is better for a five-person business?

QuickBooks is easier to model when its published bundle matches your accounting needs. ADP is worth a quote when service scope and support outweigh price transparency.

Can I compare the totals today?

Only partially. Model the verified QuickBooks inputs and leave ADP’s price fields pending until a written quote arrives.
