ADP vs Gusto: compare the quote, keep the math.

Gusto: $79/month for five people.

Simple's published subscription is $49 + $6 × 5 = $79/month, or $79 × 12 = $948/year, before selected extras. ADP RUN's public package page asks you to get pricing; it does not provide a universal rate to plug into this table. [1][2]

Normalize your ADP quote

Use the same headcount and service scope for both vendors. Enter 0 explicitly for each ADP fee the quote confirms does not apply. Do not enter zero for an unanswered question. Enter a fee in one field only; a monthly total already covering payroll must not also be entered as a per-run charge.

Your comparison assumptions

Defaults describe a five-person, 26-run scenario with no selected Gusto extras. They are editable assumptions, not your calendar or a statement that every optional service is free. Count off-cycle runs if your ADP quote charges for them. Use your actual annual count, including an extra pay period when applicable.

Amounts from your ADP RUN quote

If quoted monthly per-person fees apply, multiply them by N and include that amount in M. Include year-end forms, delivery, state, support and other selected recurring charges in the appropriate total. The model assumes unchanged rates for the year; net any confirmed first-year discount into the matching first-year inputs. For a temporary promotion or changing headcount, calculate separate periods and sum them; do not treat a temporary rate as the renewal rate.

The formulas behind the result

Let B and C be the Gusto plan's published monthly base and per-person rates, and GM, GY and GS be your entered monthly, annual and one-time Gusto extras.

Gusto first year G = 12 × (B + C × N + GM) + GY + GS
ADP first year A = 12 × M + R × (P + E × N) + Y + S
Monthly equivalent = annual total ÷ 12
First-year difference = A − G (positive means ADP costs more)
Repeat-year model = first-year total − one-time fees, only if all other rates stay unchanged

The repeat-year model is not a renewal-price prediction. Re-enter renewal rates when available. If the quote contains tiered minimums, waived periods or multiple employee groups, resolve those into totals first; a single constant rate cannot represent them accurately.

A per-run ceiling you can use when asking for a quote

For a per-run quote, the combined run charge is Q = P + E × N. Its first-year break-even ceiling is Q = (G − 12 × M − Y − S) ÷ R. A negative ceiling means fixed and annual fees already exceed the Gusto reference before any run charge. This is a price boundary for matched scope, not an ADP advertised rate.

For the five-person Simple reference only, G = $948. With explicitly assumed zero monthly, annual and setup ADP overhead, the ceilings are:

Get a quote this table can actually use

  1. Send one scope. Specify headcount, states, actual annual run count, off-cycle needs, time tracking, benefits and requested support. Ask for RUN and the named package rather than a different product's proposal.
  2. Ask for billing units. Request all monthly, per-run, per-person, annual and one-time charges; minimums; year-end forms; promotion end dates; and renewal terms. Ask the representative to mark included items explicitly.
  3. Reconcile before choosing. Sum every line into M, P, E, Y or S exactly once. Match Gusto options to the same needs, enter confirmed zeros, and export the inputs and formulas. A cheaper total with missing services is not a matched comparison.

When this comparison is useful

If you need a known single-state starting subscription, the Simple calculation gives you an auditable baseline. If you already have an ADP RUN proposal, the calculator shows what its billing units mean over your own year. If you need multiple states, select Plus or Premium before comparing. If the quote is incomplete, use the CSV as a question list; the calculator deliberately withholds a cost verdict.
