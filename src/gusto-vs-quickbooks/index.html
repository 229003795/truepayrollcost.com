Keep promotions and accounting separate

The QuickBooks page currently shows the Payroll + Simple Start bundle at $44 base/month for three months, plus $6.50 per employee, then an $88 base. With five employees: ($44 + $32.50) × 3 + ($88 + $32.50) × 9 = $1,314 for year one, assuming unchanged rates and the stated offer applies. Do not buy accounting twice if you already have it. These bundle totals do not price the Gusto scenario's benefits. [3]
