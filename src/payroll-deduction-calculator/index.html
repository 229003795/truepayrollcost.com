The deduction formula

Pre-tax-adjusted pay = gross pay − pre-tax deductions. Total listed deductions = pre-tax deductions + federal withholding + state withholding + local withholding + other employee deductions. Estimated net pay = gross pay − total listed deductions. The result is a budgeting estimate, not a filing decision or guaranteed take-home amount.

Employee deductions are not employer payroll taxes

Employee withholding comes out of the employee’s gross pay. Employer Social Security, Medicare, FUTA and state employer taxes are separate employer costs and should not be subtracted from this paycheck result. Use the employer payroll tax calculator for the employer-side FICA subtotal.

What to confirm before relying on a paycheck estimate

  • Pay frequency, gross wages and which benefits are pre-tax under the plan documents.
  • Federal withholding inputs and filing-status details used by the payroll system.
  • State and local withholding rules, reciprocity and local-tax jurisdiction.
  • Garnishments, retirement, insurance and other employee deductions.
  • Whether the payroll register or tax professional has confirmed the final withholding.

Questions people ask

Does this calculate employer payroll tax?

No. It estimates employee-side paycheck deductions; employer taxes belong in a separate employer-cost calculation.

Can I leave a withholding amount blank?

No. Enter a confirmed amount or keep the estimate pending; a missing number is not automatically zero.

Is the result guaranteed take-home pay?

No. It is a transparent estimate based on your inputs and does not replace payroll-system or tax-professional confirmation.
