How much does payroll cost?

For five billable people, Square's published full-service rate produces a $65 monthly subscription and a $780 annual subscription subtotal: ($35 + $6 × 5) × 12 = $780. This answers how much a payroll service can cost in this example. It excludes employee wages, employer taxes, benefits funding and unconfirmed extras. Your actual bill depends on the package, people billed each month and written terms.

Payroll cost per employee: use the right denominator

At constant headcount, effective monthly service cost per person = base ÷ billable people + person rate. The base is shared, not multiplied by each employee.

People billed all yearSquare monthly subscriptionAnnual subscriptionMonthly per person
1$35 + $6 = $41$41 × 12 = $492$41 ÷ 1 = $41.00
5$35 + $6 × 5 = $65$65 × 12 = $780$65 ÷ 5 = $13.00
10$35 + $6 × 10 = $95$95 × 12 = $1,140$95 ÷ 10 = $9.50

For an illustrative schedule with five people billed for six months and ten for six months, add the actual periods: ($35 + $6 × 5) × 6 + ($35 + $6 × 10) × 6 = $390 + $570 = $960. Multiplying only the final $95 month by twelve gives $1,140, overstating this schedule by $1,140 − $960 = $180.

The denominator is 5 × 6 + 10 × 6 = 90 billed person-months. $960 ÷ 90 = $10.67 per billed person-month, rounded to cents. Dividing by the final ten people does not describe each person's full-year cost. This synthetic sensitivity example is not a claim about inactive-staff billing: confirm who counts on your contract.

Assume, for illustration only, five employees each earn $30,000 gross annually. Those synthetic wage inputs give 5 × $30,000 = $150,000. Three questions about payroll costs

How much does a payroll service cost for five employees?

Square's checked full-service rate produces $65 monthly or $780 annually for five people billed all year. This is a subscription example, not a full employment budget or an average across suppliers. Selected extras and service scope can change the bill.

Does payroll cost per employee include wages and employer taxes?

Only if the stated scope includes them. A per-person subscription charge is different from employer cost per employee. Label the subtotal and period and keep unresolved taxes and benefits pending.

Should I multiply the latest payroll invoice by twelve?

Only when its rate, headcount and recurring scope apply throughout all twelve months. Split changing headcounts and promotional periods; separate annual and one-time charges before annualizing.
