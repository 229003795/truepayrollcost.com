Time card calculator with lunch

08:30–17:00 with lunch from 12:00–12:30 gives 8.00 hours if that meal is properly excluded, or 8.50 hours if it is paid. The two work segments are 210 + 270 = 480 minutes; 480 ÷ 60 = 8.00. Including the 30-minute meal gives (210 + 30 + 270) ÷ 60 = 8.50. These are illustrated clock records, assuming no clock change. A meal's name or duration alone does not establish that it may be unpaid.

Two questions: must a meal be provided, and may this time be excluded?

Those questions need separate answers. Federal guidance does not generally require meal or rest breaks under the FLSA. Short rest breaks generally count as work; bona fide meals generally do not. State obligations may add requirements. DOL official meal-and-rest-period overview · checked October 26, 2026.

For duty relief, the federal advisor asks whether the employee is completely relieved throughout the meal. Required active or inactive duties matter, including answering calls or watching equipment. It says leaving the premises is not necessary under that federal test; a state rule can be more protective. DOL official meal-period duty-relief test · checked October 26, 2026. Items to confirm before using the payroll total

  1. Actual shift dates and all worked segments, including work before or after recorded punches.
  2. Actual lunch-out and lunch-in times; distinguish a real record from a preset deduction.
  3. Whether the employee was fully relieved, with any interruptions or required duties documented.
  4. The work state, applicable industry and employee rules, and any valid exception or waiver.
  5. Meal timing and length, plus any second-meal issue for a longer shift.
  6. Contract and policy promises about paid meals or breaks.
  7. Clock-change accuracy, payroll review and the evidence retained with the time record.

Three questions before deducting lunch

Can I automatically subtract thirty minutes for lunch?

No. A preset duration does not show what actually happened or whether the time may be excluded. Confirm the actual interval, duties and applicable requirements first; unresolved facts stay pending.

Does a required meal period automatically mean an unpaid meal?

No. A requirement to provide a meal and the treatment of its time are separate questions. Check both the governing meal rule and whether this particular interval is compensable.

Can I calculate an overnight lunch timecard?

Yes, by entering the actual next date for lunch or shift end. This calculator uses clock minutes without time-zone or daylight-saving adjustment; independently correct a clock-change record before relying on its total.
