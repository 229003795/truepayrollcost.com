What this federal estimate includes

For 2026, employer Social Security is 6.2% of covered wages up to the $184,500 wage base, and employer Medicare is 1.45% with no wage base limit. FUTA is entered separately because the credit and state account facts can change the amount.

What it does not include

State unemployment, state payroll taxes, local taxes, workers' compensation and benefits are not invented here. Keep them as pending until the applicable state and employer facts are confirmed. This is employer-side cost, not employee withholding; use the payroll deduction calculator for the employee side.

Questions people ask

Does this include employee withholding?

No. It estimates the employer share only.

Why is FUTA an input?

The applicable FUTA amount depends on credit and state account facts, so an unconfirmed number is not silently assumed.

Does this show total employer cost?

No. State, local, benefits and other employer costs remain separate until confirmed.
