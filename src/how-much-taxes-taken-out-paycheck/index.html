What this estimate includes

This tool adds the federal, state and local income-tax lines you enter from a pay stub. It does not decide the correct W-4 amount, reproduce IRS percentage tables, or estimate FICA unless you enter those deductions separately.

Do not confuse employee withholding with employer payroll tax

These are employee-side deductions from gross pay. Employer Social Security, Medicare, FUTA and other employer costs are separate; use the employer payroll tax calculator for that side. For a broader deduction list, use the payroll deduction calculator.

Check the pay stub before relying on the annual number

  • Use the actual pay frequency: 24 semi-monthly checks and 26 biweekly checks are different.
  • Read federal, state and local lines separately; do not combine a state or city line with employer taxes.
  • Bonuses, benefits, W-4 changes and local rules can change a later paycheck.
  • For federal withholding calculations, compare the payroll system with IRS Publication 15-T.

Questions people ask

Is this the same as employer payroll tax?

No. It totals employee-side withholding entered from the paycheck.

Why does my annual total differ from this estimate?

Pay frequency, bonuses, benefit deductions and changing withholding can change the actual year total.

Should I add Social Security and Medicare?

Only if you want them included and enter those employee deductions separately; this page does not invent them.
