What this does and does not decide

The gross payment is target net plus the withholding amount you enter. Employer Social Security is 6.2% of covered wages up to the applicable wage base; employer Medicare is 1.45% with no wage-base limit. State taxes, FUTA, benefits, and any unconfirmed withholding remain pending until the applicable facts are known.

This page does not run the IRS percentage method, determine Form W-4 entries, or promise a final tax amount. Use confirmed payroll inputs and the applicable payroll guidance before paying.

Questions people ask

Does gross-up calculate withholding?

No. It uses the withholding amount you confirm; it does not decide the IRS percentage-method result.

Does the result include every employer cost?

No. FUTA, state and local taxes, benefits, and other costs stay separate until confirmed.

What is the employer payroll-tax portion?

The example applies 6.2% employer Social Security and 1.45% employer Medicare to the gross payment.
