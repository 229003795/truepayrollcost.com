Paychex pricing: what it costs and what Paychex does not publish

Paychex publishes no rate card. Every pricing page on paychex.com resolves to a quote request, so there is no official Paychex dollar figure to repeat. What Paychex does publish, checked October 27, 2026, is a range: $30–$100 per employee per month for outsourced payroll, and $30–$100 per month for a single employee. Paychex Flex itself has four tiers — Basic, Foundations, Advanced and Complete — and each tier's price reads Request Pricing. Official Paychex sources

That is why this page does not show a Paychex price table. Nine of the ten pages ranking for this keyword show a price; none of them can be checked against Paychex, because Paychex publishes none. What you can do instead is rebuild the quote from the billing units Paychex actually charges, and hold the written quote to the ranges Paychex states in its own words.

Rebuild the Paychex quote from its billing units

Because Paychex does not publish rates, the quote arrives as amounts without visible derivation. Ask for each of these separately, then fill the blank. A quote with one bundled monthly number and no unit breakdown is the thing to push back on.

Paychex's own articles make two of these points explicitly: pay frequency changes the bill, and extras such as direct deposit and tax filing add to it. Ask which of these are already inside your tier before counting them again.

Work the ranges into a number you can check

These examples use Paychex's own published ranges, not an assumed Paychex price. They show the size of the question you are asking, so you can see whether a quote is plausible.

Example A: ten billable people across one year, at both ends of Paychex's stated range

  • Low end: $30 × 10 people × 12 months = $3,600/year.
  • High end: $100 × 10 people × 12 months = $12,000/year.
  • Monthly equivalent: $300 at the low end, $1,000 at the high end.

A ten-person quoted number far outside $300–$1,000 per month is worth questioning — not because the range is a promise, but because it is Paychex's own stated market band. Official price source · checked October 27, 2026

Example B: one employee, one year

  • Low end: $30 × 1 × 12 = $360/year.
  • High end: $100 × 1 × 12 = $1,200/year.

This is the single-employee case Paychex publishes its own range for, and it is the case most often quoted as a flat monthly number with nothing else attached. Confirm whether the figure includes tax filing, direct deposit and the year-end form processing.

Example C: the same range applied to total cost, not to the software

Paychex states total payroll cost can run 1.25×–1.4× base pay. At an employee's $30/hour base pay, that is $37.50–$42/hour total. For 2,000 hours in a year at $30/hour, base wages are $60,000, so the total cost band is $75,000–$84,000. That band covers wages, payroll taxes, benefits and the payroll service together — it is not a software price, and the software is only one line inside it. Four questions to ask before signing

  1. Which tier is this quote for, and what does the next tier add? Paychex marks Foundations "Best Value". Ask for the difference in dollars per month, not in feature adjectives.
  2. Is the per-employee charge monthly or annual, and does it count W-2 and 1099 recipients? This single question changes the annual total more than any feature choice.
  3. What is the price in month 13? Promotional or introductory pricing on the first period is common across payroll vendors. Ask for the standard rate that applies from the first renewal, in writing.
  4. What does cancelling cost, and how much notice is required? The notice period sets the overlap you will actually pay for if you switch providers mid-year.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Paychex cost?

Paychex publishes no rate card, so there is no single official price to quote. Its own stated range is $30–$100 per employee per month for outsourced payroll, and $30–$100 per month for a single employee, both checked October 27, 2026. Your actual cost comes from the written quote, which you can check against that band.

Does Paychex have a published price for one employee?

No. The single-employee page states a $30–$100 per month range depending on software versus full service, and notes that weekly runs often cost more than bi-weekly or monthly. For a real one-person quote, ask for the base, the per-person charge and whether tax filing and direct deposit are inside it.

Is $29/month Paychex pricing?

No. $29/month plus $7 per worker is SurePayroll's published card. SurePayroll's own footer identifies it as "A Paychex Company", so it is a Paychex product with its own price — but it is not the Paychex Flex rate card, and the two should not be quoted interchangeably.

How do I compare a quote-based vendor like Paychex against Gusto or QuickBooks?

Convert the Paychex quote into the same units as the published competitors first: base per month, per-employee per month, and any one-time or annual lines. Then run the same headcount and duration through each. A monthly figure compared with an annual figure will make one vendor look wrong when neither is.

What should I ask Paychex in writing before accepting a quote?

Which tier the quote covers, whether the per-employee charge is monthly or annual and whether it counts 1099 recipients, the standard rate that applies from the first renewal, and the cancellation notice period. Ask for each as a written line, then reconcile the total against Paychex's own stated range.
