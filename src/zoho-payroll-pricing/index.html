Zoho Payroll pricing: keep the US payroll plan separate from Zoho People

The first check is product scope, not a headline number. Zoho publishes a US Payroll pricing page and a separate pricing-comparison page. Do not copy Zoho People or People Plus rates into a payroll bill. Record the plan, employee billing basis, tax filing scope and any annual commitment exactly as quoted.

Zoho Payroll quote checklist

  • Copy the plan name and the US region from the official pricing page.

  • List tax filing jurisdictions, year-end forms, support and implementation separately.

FAQ

Is Zoho People pricing the same as Zoho Payroll pricing?

No. They are separate products and must be recorded separately.

Can I use a Zoho People per-user rate as a payroll estimate?

No. Wait for the US Zoho Payroll plan and billing basis shown in the official payroll pricing flow.

What should an employer confirm before paying?

Confirm employee count, jurisdictions, filing scope, billing term, support and add-ons; leave any unavailable amount pending.
