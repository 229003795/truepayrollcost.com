Justworks pricing: Payroll, PEO Basic and PEO Plus

For five people, published Payroll pricing is $90 per month, or $1,080 for twelve months. That is $50 base plus $8 × 5 employees; PEO Basic and PEO Plus use different per-employee rates and have no base fee.

What stays outside the subscription subtotal

Justworks lists add-ons such as time tracking at $8 per employee per month, international contractors at $39 per paid contractor per month, EOR at $599 per employee per month, dedicated HR consulting at $30 per employee per month, and CHOICE health reimbursements at $25 per enrolled employee per month. Keep these separate from the plan subtotal unless the written quote confirms they apply.

The official page says there is no implementation fee. Insurance, workers’ compensation, statutory disability, benefits, payroll taxes and other administrative or annual fees can still affect the total bill; do not silently add or treat them as zero.

Quote checklist

  • Whether the quote is Payroll, PEO Basic or PEO Plus—not a mixed product.
  • Active W-2 employees, paid owners, part-time employees, contractors and vendors.
  • States, workers’ compensation, insurance and benefits selected.
  • Add-ons, annual fees, payroll taxes, prepayment terms and renewal language.
  • Whether the five-person example matches the headcount and billing month in the quote.

FAQ

What is Justworks Payroll for five people?

The published subtotal is $90 per month, or $1,080 for twelve months before add-ons and other costs.

How is PEO Basic different in the price math?

PEO Basic is $79 per employee per month with no base fee, so five people produce a $4,740 annual subscription subtotal.

Are these complete employer costs?

No. They are plan subtotals. Benefits, insurance, workers’ compensation, taxes, add-ons and other fees must be confirmed separately.
