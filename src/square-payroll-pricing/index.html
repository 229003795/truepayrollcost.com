Square Payroll pricing: the published rate, annualized

Square publishes a flat rate card: full-service payroll is $35/month + $6 per person paid per month, checked October 27, 2026. Unlike a quote-only vendor, this is a number you can turn into an annual cost today. Annualized for N billable people it is ($35 + $6×N) × 12 = $420 + $72×N per year — the same base-plus-per-person caliber the other pages in this series use — and it can be set directly against Paychex's quote-only range.

Separately charged items sit outside the $35 + $6 base. 401(k) retirement is offered through Square's partner Vestwell: Square's own 401(k) page states their most popular plan is $125/mo + $8 per participating employee, with participants paying an annual account fee starting at 0.2%. Workers' compensation and health insurance have no published price from Square and are quoted on request, and paid amendment services are an add-on with no published price. None of those separately charged items is inside the $35 + $6 figure. Timecard import and scheduling (up to 10 days out) are the exception: they are included in full-service payroll, so time tracking is not a separate charge.

Annualize it in the same caliber as the other pages

The batch pages express cost as base × 12 + per-person rate × headcount × 12. Square fits that exactly: the $35 base recurs every month and the $6 person fee recurs per billable person per month.

Billable people Monthly total Annual subscription total Per-person monthly cost
1 $35 + $6 = $41 $492 $41.00
5 $35 + $30 = $65 $780 $13.00
10 $35 + $60 = $95 $1,140 $9.50
25 $35 + $150 = $185 $2,220 $7.40

The arithmetic is ($35 + $6×N) × 12. A contractor-only team pays no base: $6 × N × 12 = $72×N per year (10 contractors = $720/year). These are subscription subtotals from the dated official rates — they exclude wages, employer payroll taxes, benefits funding and any opted-in paper-W-2 fee ($3 per mailed form per year).

Watch the per-person number. Because Square's $35 base is fixed, per-person cost falls as headcount rises: $41 per person at one employee drops to $9.50 per person at ten. Compare per-person figures only at the same headcount, or the comparison is meaningless.

The annualization mistake to avoid

Three ways this comparison goes wrong:

  • Mixing monthly and annual. Comparing Square's $95 monthly (10 people) against a Paychex annual figure, or annualizing a single Square month as the whole year, makes one vendor look wrong when neither is. Keep both sides annual and at the same headcount.
  • Treating Paychex's range as a quote. It is Paychex's description of the market, not a price it will charge you. Use it as a band to sanity-check a real quote, not as a competitor price.
  • Inventing a Square promotion. The official pricing page shows no introductory discount, so there is no "first-month" number to annualize. Referral "first month free" offers appear off the official page and are unverified — confirm any promo in writing before using it.

Items to confirm before signing

  1. Product identity: Is the quote full-service payroll, or contractor-only at $6 with no base? Record the exact plan.
  2. Who counts as a billable person: Employees, contractors, or both — and whether inactive staff are billed. Square bills people actually paid in a month.
  3. Tax filing scope: Confirm federal and state filings are included (they are on the published page) and which local jurisdictions Square files for your states.
  4. Add-ons: Which of 401(k), benefits or amendments you selected, and for each one whether the price is published by Square or has to come back in writing as a quote. 401(k) sits on Vestwell's plan price of $125/mo + $8 per participating employee; timecard import and scheduling are already inside the full-service plan.
  5. Paper forms: If you opt into mailed W-2/1099 copies, budget $3 per mailed form per year.
  6. Renewal: No long-term contract is advertised; confirm there is no auto-escalation clause and what the rate is from month 13.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Square Payroll cost?

Full-service payroll is $35/month + $6 per person paid per month, checked October 27, 2026. Annualized for N billable people that is ($35 + $6×N) × 12. Contractor-only payroll is $6 per person per month with no base fee. Federal and state tax filings and multistate payroll are included; timecard import and scheduling are also included in full-service payroll, and mailed paper W-2/1099 copies are extra at $3 per mailed form per year. 401(k) retirement is offered through partner Vestwell at $125/mo + $8 per participating employee on Square's own 401(k) page, and workers' compensation, health insurance and paid amendment services are quoted on request.

Does the $35 base include tax filing?

Yes. Square's published page includes automated federal and state tax payments and filings, plus digital W-2/1099 delivery, in the full-service $35 + $6 price. Paper mailed copies are the only tax-form line that costs extra, at $3 per mailed form per year.

What should I confirm before accepting a Square quote?

The exact plan (full-service vs contractor-only), who counts as a billable person, that tax filing and multistate are included, each selected add-on and its rate, any opted-in paper-W-2 fee, and the month-13 renewal rate. Enter each on the worksheet and keep unknown lines marked "awaiting quote."
