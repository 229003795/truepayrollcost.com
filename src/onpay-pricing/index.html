OnPay pricing: turn the worker count into an annual bill

Five billable workers on Payroll Essentials cost $79 per month and $948 for twelve regular-price months: ($49 + $6 × 5) × 12 = $948. This is a subscription subtotal, before applicable sales tax, selected extras and benefit funding. It does not deduct an introductory offer. Count workers included in pay runs, not just people receiving positive pay.

The billable-worker test: zero wages can still create a charge

The OnPay official billing, form-delivery and sales-tax FAQ · checked October 29, 2026 says inclusion in a pay run can trigger the worker fee even with no earnings. Repeated runs do not multiply the monthly person charge. Self-printing forms is free; optional direct mailing is $10 per form. Applicable subscription sales tax is separate.

Make a monthly evidence row for each worker. Record whether the person was included in any run, whether pay was zero, and the invoice cycle. Someone appearing in several runs is still one worker in that month's count. Someone absent from every run should not be casually assumed billable: reconcile their invoice treatment with OnPay.

A month-by-month reconciliation you can reuse

The annual equation is: sum of each month's base plus person rate × distinct included workers, plus selected annual or per-form charges. Keep a record of billing periods rather than treating a calendar-year wage total as proof of the software bill.

  1. Match invoice period and selected product scope.
  2. Count distinct included workers, including any zero-pay entries. Keep run evidence without putting employee personal information in a public document.
  3. Apply that period's published or contracted price. A midyear product change requires a new row.
  4. Keep form delivery, tax and other confirmed charges separate; never count an included service twice.
  5. Reconcile the subtotal to the invoice. Leave unexplained differences pending instead of forcing a balancing zero.

Three pricing questions

How much does OnPay cost for five workers?

Payroll Essentials is $79 monthly or $948 over twelve regular-price months at the checked rates. Selected products, direct-mailed forms and applicable sales tax can add to that subtotal; an offer is not automatically deducted.

Does an employee paid nothing count toward OnPay pricing?

Yes, if included in a pay run during the billing period, according to OnPay's billing FAQ. Check the inclusion record rather than using positive wages alone as your headcount test.

Are W-2 and 1099 forms included?

Filing and self-print access are included, while optional direct mailing is listed at $10 per form. Record the number of forms actually selected for mailing; do not add a second filing fee for an included service.
