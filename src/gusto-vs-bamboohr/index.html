Why the boundary decides the comparison

Gusto is a payroll product with HR built on top of it. Its official pricing page puts unlimited payrolls, tax filings and payments into every plan, then adds HR guidance, time tracking and scheduling on the higher tiers. The five-worker figure above is a payroll-inclusive subscription number.

BambooHR is a human-resources information system that also markets payroll inside its platform, but it publishes no payroll price; payroll is arranged through a demo/quote rather than a posted rate. Its official pricing page sells Core, Pro and Elite as per-employee HR plans with Get Quote actions and automatic volume discounts. Payroll has its own official product page describing US-based tax filing and a Request a Demo action, but no published price. So the HR list rate and the Gusto subscription rate answer different questions: one prices the employee record and people processes, the other prices paying people and filing their taxes.

We therefore do not put the two list rates into one ranking. A lower or higher number on one side would not settle the same job, and the side with no published payroll price cannot be given a total at all.

Which one fits your operating problem?

Choose the payroll-first product when payroll and tax filing are the purchase and you want the whole subscription visible before a sales call. The five-worker subtotal is calculable from published rates, and Simple, Plus and Premium are separated by state coverage and HR/time features rather than by a hidden quote.

Choose the HRIS when payroll is already handled elsewhere and the job is maintaining employee records, onboarding, time-off policy, reviews or compensation cycles. Payroll can then be arranged through a demo/quote if you want it in the same system, or kept with your existing payroll arrangement.

Confirmation checklist

  • Count active employees and contractor months separately; the per-person charge applies to billable people.
  • Write down every state and local jurisdiction that needs registration or filing before choosing an entry plan.
  • Ask for the HR plan quote in writing, including the asterisk conditions and the volume discount that applies at your headcount.
  • Ask for payroll as its own line: price, implementation, multi-state filing, year-end forms, and whether it is an add-on or a separate subscription.
  • Verify which HR and time modules are in the plan you are quoted rather than the ones on a higher tier.
  • Keep wages, employer taxes, benefit premiums and internal administration outside the software-subscription subtotal.
  • Re-check the official prices on the day you buy; this page records the figures published on the checked date.

FAQ

Is BambooHR a payroll product in the same sense as Gusto?

No. Gusto's official pricing page includes payroll and tax filings and payments on every plan. BambooHR's official pricing page publishes HR plans with Get Quote actions; payroll is marketed on its own page with no published price and is arranged through a demo/quote.

Can I put both into one five-worker annual table?

Not as a like-for-like cost. Only one side has a fully published subscription formula for payroll plus HR. The other side's published per-employee rate covers the HR system and excludes payroll, so an annualized number would compare different scopes.

Does BambooHR publish a payroll price?

Not on the official pages checked on November 27, 2026. Request it in writing. Referral or bundle discounts are account-specific and are not used in the math on this page.

Which option is cheaper for five workers?

This page does not judge. Price level cannot be decided when one side's payroll cost is only available in a quote. The decision here is about product range and scope.
