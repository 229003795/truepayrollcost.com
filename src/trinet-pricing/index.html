TriNet pricing: PEPM model and quote checklist

TriNet prices its PEO and HR Plus services per employee per month, but does not publish one universal dollar rate. The rate varies by region, industry, company size and selected services, so use the PEPM unit without inventing a quote.

PEO and HR Plus are different scopes

TriNet PEO can include payroll tax administration, benefits and workers’ compensation administration under TriNet’s PEO structure. HR Plus is an administrative-services model where the business remains the employer of record. Do not compare their PEPM figures until the scope and responsibility for taxes, benefits and insurance are aligned.

TriNet quote checklist

  • PEO or HR Plus, employee count and any minimum worksite count.
  • States, worksites, industry classification and payroll frequency.
  • Payroll tax filing, workers’ compensation, benefits and insurance lines.
  • HR consulting, onboarding, time, reporting and integrations.
  • Implementation, annual agreement, renewal, cancellation and transaction fees.

FAQ

How much does TriNet cost?

TriNet does not publish one universal dollar rate; pricing is customized on a PEPM basis.

Can I calculate a TriNet annual price from PEPM?

Yes, once the written PEPM rate is confirmed: employees × PEPM × months. The $3,000 example above is a fixture, not a TriNet quote.

Does PEPM include benefits and workers’ compensation?

Not automatically for every scope. Confirm each line and whether it is included, itemized or separately billed.
