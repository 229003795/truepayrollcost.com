Wave Payroll pricing: current US cost, annualized

For five active employees, Wave's published full-access US payroll subscription is $70 per month, or $840 for twelve months. The formula is $40 base + ($6 × 5 active employees). This is a subscription subtotal, not a complete employer bill.

What the $840 does and does not answer

  • It annualizes the published subscription only: base fee plus five active-employee fees.
  • It does not add wages, employer taxes, sales tax, contractor payments, optional services or internal administration time.
  • If a business pauses payroll, Wave documents a separate limited-access subscription of $17 per month; that is a different scenario, not a discount applied to the $840 example.
  • Wave's first monthly bill can show the base fee while prior-month active-employee and contractor fees are billed retroactively.

FAQ

What is Wave Payroll's five-employee annual subscription example?

$40 plus $6 for each of five active employees is $70 per month, or $840 for twelve months.

Does the $840 include contractor charges?

No. Wave lists a separate $6 fee for each independent contractor paid, so contractor charges depend on the month's payments.

Is $840 the complete employer payroll cost?

No. It is the published subscription subtotal. Wages, employer taxes, sales tax, optional services and internal time remain separate or pending.
