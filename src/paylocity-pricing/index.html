Paylocity pricing: what the official quote includes

Paylocity does not publish a dollar price on its official pricing page; it says pricing is tailored to the organization and asks buyers to request pricing. Treat any third-party dollar figure as unverified until it appears in your written quote.

What the official page says to confirm

Quote linePublicly described?What to request
Payroll platformYesPer-employee basis, payroll frequency and included runs
Tax filing and complianceYesFederal, state and local filing scope and exclusions

Paylocity’s pricing shape

Paylocity’s small-business page says it charges per employee per month rather than per payroll run. That describes the billing unit, not a public rate. The official pricing page also presents payroll, HR, workforce management, benefits, Finance and IT as selectable modules, so a payroll-only comparison should not silently treat the full platform as included.

Quote checklist

  • Number of employees, contractors and entities used for the quote.
  • Payroll frequency, states and local jurisdictions.
  • Tax filing, payment, year-end forms and compliance support.
  • Time, scheduling, benefits, HR, Finance, IT and integration modules.
  • Implementation, data migration, support, promotion, renewal and termination terms.

FAQ

How much does Paylocity cost?

The official public page does not state a dollar amount; request a written quote.

Is Paylocity priced per employee?

Paylocity’s small-business page describes per-employee-per-month billing rather than per-payroll-run billing, but it does not publish the rate.

Can I compare a Paylocity quote with a flat-rate provider?

Yes, but separate subscription, implementation, filing, module, support and renewal lines before comparing annual totals.
