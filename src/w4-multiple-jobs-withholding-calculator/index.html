How the split works

The annual shortfall is divided between jobs by the percentage you choose, then each job's share is divided by that job's remaining paydays. The IRS says you may apply the extra amount to one job or divide it between jobs; each job receiving a change needs a new Form W-4.

What this does not decide

This page does not calculate the annual tax shortfall, run IRS withholding tables or decide filing status, credits or multiple-job adjustments. Use the IRS estimator or Publication 505 for those inputs.

This is employee withholding, not employer payroll tax. For a single-job Step 4(c) division use the Step 4(c) calculator.

Questions people ask

Can I put all extra withholding on one job?

Yes. The IRS allows applying it to one job or dividing it between jobs.

Why are the two per-check amounts different?

Each amount uses that job's own remaining paydays.

Does this replace the Multiple Jobs Worksheet?

No. It only allocates an annual amount you already estimated.
