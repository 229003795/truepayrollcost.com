ADP payroll pricing: published rates and quote-only plans

ADP publishes $500 per year for ADP for One, billed annually for one-person payroll; its RUN payroll packages and larger payroll plans direct buyers to Get Pricing. Do not multiply the one-person offer into a general ADP rate.

What changes the ADP quote

ADP’s own cost guidance lists payroll frequency, employee count, services, payee changes and number of states as pricing factors. It also warns that direct deposit, check delivery, tax filing, year-end forms and new-hire reporting may be separate or package-dependent. Keep each line visible in a written quote.

ADP pricing quote checklist

  • Product name: ADP for One, RUN package, Workforce Now or another solution.
  • Employees, contractors, entities, pay frequency and states.
  • Tax filing, W-2/1099, SUI, garnishments and new-hire reporting.
  • Direct deposit, checks, time, HR, benefits, retirement and integrations.
  • Setup, implementation, promotional months, renewal and cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much is ADP payroll?

ADP for One publishes $500 annually for one-person payroll; RUN and larger plans require a quote.

Can I use the $500 price for five employees?

No. ADP identifies that offer as ADP for One; do not extrapolate it to RUN or multi-employee plans.

Does ADP payroll pricing include tax filing?

RUN package pages describe tax filing, but the exact inclusion and fees belong in the written quote.
