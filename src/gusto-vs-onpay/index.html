Gusto vs OnPay: same entry math, different operating fit

At the published entry rate, five workers cost $79 per month on either service: $49 base + ($6 × 5). Annualized, that is $948 before taxes, benefits, add-ons, or internal time. The practical choice is scope, not a made-up price winner.

Which one fits the actual operating problem?

Choose Gusto Simple when single-state payroll and a broader upgrade path for HR, time, and employee tools matter more than multi-state payroll in the base plan. The official plan page describes Simple as single-state payroll; Plus and Premium add broader payroll and HR capabilities.

Choose OnPay when one payroll plan with multi-state filing, year-end forms, and payroll-specialist support is the cleaner operating brief. OnPay’s official pricing page says its base plan includes multi-state payroll and unlimited pay runs; optional HR and time bundles remain separate.

The related Gusto vs BambooHR question

Gusto vs BambooHR is not the same product comparison. Gusto publishes payroll plans; BambooHR is an HR platform whose payroll availability and price depend on the product configuration and sales process. Do not compare a BambooHR platform quote to Gusto’s $49 + $6 entry math as if they were the same SKU. Confirm whether the BambooHR quote includes payroll, implementation, tax filing, and the states you need.

Confirmation checklist

  • Count active W-2 employees and contractor months separately.
  • Write down every state and local jurisdiction requiring registration or filing.
  • Confirm whether time tracking, HR, benefits, workers’ compensation, and year-end delivery are included or add-ons.
  • Save the quote date; promotions and renewal terms can change the annual subtotal.
  • Keep wages, employer taxes, benefit premiums, and internal administration outside this software-subscription comparison.

FAQ

Are Gusto and OnPay the same price for five workers?

At the published entry rates checked November 7, 2026, both calculate to $79 per month and $948 for twelve months before extras.

Which service includes multi-state payroll in the base comparison?

OnPay’s official pricing page states that multi-state payroll is included; Gusto Simple is described as single-state payroll, with broader capabilities on higher plans.

Is Gusto vs BambooHR a direct payroll-price comparison?

No. BambooHR is a broader HR platform and the payroll scope and price must be confirmed in the quote. Keep that request separate from the published Gusto and OnPay entry math.
