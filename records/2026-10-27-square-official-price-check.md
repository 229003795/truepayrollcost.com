## Official facts — Square (read 2026-10-27)

Source: `https://squareup.com/us/en/payroll/pricing` (and corroborating Square pages).

| Item | Official figure |
| --- | --- |
| Full-service payroll | **$35/month + $6 per person paid per month** |
| Contractor-only payroll | **$6 per person paid per month** (no base fee if only contractors) |
| Federal & state payroll tax filings (quarterly + annual W-2/1099-NEC) | **Included**, no extra charge |
| Multistate payroll | **Included** in full-service |
| Unlimited pay runs | Included |
| Paper W-2 / 1099 copies mailed | **$3 per mailed form per year** (opt-in) |
| Contract / commitment | **No long-term contract** |
| Digital W-2/1099 delivery | Included |

Add-ons (from Merchant Maverick; presented as separate, **not** included):

- 401(k) retirement: **$49/month + $8/employee**
- Time tracking: **$2.50–$4.00/month per person**
- Health insurance, workers' compensation, paid amendment services: **varies / add-on**

## Outdated figure note

One Square "townsquare" article still shows legacy "$29/month + $5 per employee." That content is older
than the current official pricing page, which states **$35 + $6**. Use $35 + $6 as the checked
2026-10-27 figure; treat $29/$5 as stale.

## Annualization (batch caliber: base × 12 + per-person × headcount × 12)

Full-service, N billable people, 12 months: **($35 + $6×N) × 12 = $420 + $72×N per year.**

- N = 1: **$492/year** ($41/mo)
- N = 5: **$780/year** ($65/mo)
- N = 10: **$1,140/year** ($95/mo)
- N = 25: **$2,220/year** ($185/mo)

Contractor-only, N: **$6×N×12 = $72×N per year.** Paper W-2/1099: **+$3 × forms/year.**

## Head-to-head basis vs Paychex

Paychex (checked 2026-10-27) publishes **no rate card**; its own stated market range is **$30–$100 per
employee per month** for outsourced payroll → **$360–$1,200 per employee per year**, or **$360N–$1,200N
total per year** for N people. Square's published totals sit at or below the bottom of that band at
every headcount shown (e.g., 10 people: Square **$1,140** vs Paychex stated **$3,600–$12,000**).
**Caveat:** Paychex's band is a market-range statement, not a Paychex quote; only a written Paychex
quote can say what Paychex would actually charge the same ten people.
