## What the audit shows about this SERP

1. **Paychex's own pages hold 5 of the top 13 organic positions** (1, 7, 10, 11, 12). The official
   Compare Payroll Packages page is #1.
2. **Every one of Paychex's own pricing pages shows no number.** They are quote forms. The SERP is
   therefore full of third-party pages *guessing* at a price Paychex will not publish.
3. **A competitor's editorial is in the top 5** (gusto.com, position 4), publishing an explainer about
   Paychex pricing — i.e. a rival vendor is already ranking for our exact target keyword.
4. **SurePayroll (position 13) is the only Paychex-owned page with a public rate card.** It is also a
   target keyword on its own (`surepayroll pricing`, already covered by a later batch item).
5. **Reddit (position 5) with 50+ comments** outranks most vendor pages. Real-operator cost experience
   outranks vendor marketing on this query.

## Content implication for /paychex-pricing/

The gap this page must fill is **not another guessed price table** — nine of the top results already
supply that, and none of them is right in a way the reader can check. The gap is:

- A dated statement of **what Paychex actually publishes and what it refuses to publish**, checked on a
  stated date against paychex.com itself.
- The **billing units** a Paychex quote is actually built from, so a reader can rebuild the quote.
- Paychex's own **published market ranges** ($30–$100 per employee per month; $30–$100 per month for a
  single employee; 1.25×–1.4× of base pay), which are official and dated.
- A **written-quote confirmation checklist**, because with a quote-based vendor the artifact that
  resolves the number is a signed scope, not a webpage.
- The **one official Paychex rate card that does exist** (SurePayroll, "A Paychex Company"), clearly
  labelled as a different product so it is never read as Paychex Flex pricing.
