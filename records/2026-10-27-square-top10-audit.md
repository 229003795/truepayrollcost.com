## Method note (read this first)

The prior Paychex audit (`records/2026-10-27-paychex-top10-audit.md`) used the logged-in Google
Chrome profile (`chegrogo@gmail.com`) with `hl=en&gl=us&pws=0`. In this session the bsk browser
extension would not re-attach to a running Chrome instance: after launch, `bsk browsers` reported
**0 browsers connected**, and `bsk doctor` reported `extension connected — 0 browsers`. The live
logged-in SERP could not be captured.

Per the browser-skill's documented bounded-recovery rule — *"If this skill cannot attach to the
existing Chrome instance after its documented bounded recovery, preserve the user's tabs and report
the exact attachment limitation. Continue independent non-browser work"* — the audit below was
compiled from web-search observations on 2026-10-27 instead. **Positions are one observation on one
date and approximate the organic SERP; they are not a verified logged-in Chrome capture.** The
analytical conclusions hold regardless of method: Square's own pages dominate the SERP and they
publish the price, so the gap is annualization + a Paychex head-to-head, not "Square won't tell you."

## What the audit shows about this SERP

1. **Square's own pages hold 3 of the top 10 organic positions** (1, 2, 6), and they **do publish the
   price** ($35 + $6). Unlike the Paychex SERP — where every official page is a quote form — here the
   number is one click away.
2. **The published price is repeated, not annualized.** Every review and roundup echoes "$35 + $6 per
   person," but none converts it into the same base + per-person × 12 annual caliber the batch pages
   use, and none lines it up against quote-only vendors like Paychex in that caliber.
3. **A competitor comparison dominates the mid-SERP** (positions 6–7 Square vs Gusto; 8–10 multi-vendor
   roundups). Square vs Gusto is the most-fought comparison keyword.
4. **No page does the Paychex head-to-head.** Searches for "square payroll pricing" return
   Square-vs-Gusto and generic roundups, but nothing puts Square's published annual cost next to
   Paychex's quote-only range in the same units — which is exactly the gap this page fills.

## Content implication for /square-payroll-pricing/

The gap is **not** "Square won't tell you the price" (it does). The gap is:

- **Annualize Square's published rate** into the same base + per-person × 12 caliber the batch pages
  use, with worked headcounts.
- **Head-to-head with Paychex's quote-only range**, so a reader who searched "paychex pricing" can
  walk two steps to a published-number comparison.
- Keep the same **included vs extra** discipline (tax filing included; 401(k), time tracking and
  paper W-2 are extras).
