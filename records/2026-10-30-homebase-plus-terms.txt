6 MONTHS FREE PAYROLL & PLUS OFFER
TERMS AND CONDITIONS

This promotion (the “Offer”) is valid for new Homebase customers who complete payroll setup by 12/15/2026 and run their first payroll between 9/15/2026 and 1/20/2027 (the “Offer Period”). The Offer applies to all locations added during this time. Customers must complete checkout with a valid credit card after the 14-day Homebase free trial.

The Plus plan is free for 6 months starting at checkout, and Payroll is free for 6 months beginning with the first payroll run made on or before 1/20/2027. Payroll is free for up to 50 employees, with $6/month per employee thereafter. If payroll is not run by 1/20/2027, the Offer does not apply and standard pricing begins.
