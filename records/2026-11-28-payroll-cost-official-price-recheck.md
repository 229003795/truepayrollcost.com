## Result

The published base and per-person rates for the four plans already cited on the page were unchanged from the 2026-10-28 check. The article was updated to show all four five-person annual package subtotals rather than only two:

| Plan | Five-person calculation | Annual subscription subtotal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square full-service | `($35 + $6 × 5) × 12` | `$780` |
| Gusto Simple | `($49 + $6 × 5) × 12` | `$948` |
| Gusto Plus | `($80 + $12 × 5) × 12` | `$1,680` |
| Gusto Premium | `($180 + $22 × 5) × 12` | `$3,480` |

The annual figures are our arithmetic on published rates. They are package subscription subtotals only: they exclude wages, employer taxes, benefit funding, setup and any selected add-ons, and they are not a claim that the four plans cover identical work.
